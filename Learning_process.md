# Learning Process

## What is the Feynman Technique?

The Feynman Technique is a way of learning by explaining a topic in simple words. If I can explain it clearly to someone else, it means I understand it well.

## What was the most interesting story or idea for you?

The idea that I liked the most is **Practice vs. Understanding**.

- I learned that solving many questions is useful, but it is more important to understand why the answer is correct. When I understand the concept, I can solve different types of problems instead of just remembering the solution.

## What are active and diffused modes of thinking?

### Focused Mode

I give my full attention to one task and solve it without distractions.

### Diffuse Mode

When I take a short break or relax, my brain connects ideas and sometimes helps me find a better solution.

## What are the steps to take when approaching a new topic?

When I start learning something new, I follow these steps:

1. Learn the basics first.
2. Avoid distractions.
3. Break the topic into small parts.
4. Practice regularly.

## What are some of the actions you can take going forward to improve your learning process?

- Focus on one task at a time.
- Study in a distraction-free place.
- Focus on one task at a time.
- Explain what I learned in my own words.
- Practice by solving problems or writing code.
- Keep learning in a simple and enjoyable way.
