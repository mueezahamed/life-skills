
# Listening and Active Communication


## 1. Active Listening  

**Question 1:** What are the steps/strategies to do Active Listening? (Minimum 6 points)  

**Answer:**  
To be an active listener, I should:  
- Give full attention to the person who is speaking.
- Concentrate on what the speaker is saying.
- Avoid interrupting while the person is talking.
- Allow the speaker to complete their point before I respond.
- Repeat or summarize what I understood in my own words.
- Keep an open mind and not judge the person too quickly.  


## 2. Reflective Listening  

**Question 2:** According to Fisher's model, what are the key points of Reflective Listening?  

**Answer:**  
- Listen properly instead of thinking about what I will say next.
- Repeat or explain in my own words what the other person has said.
- Make sure that I have understood the speaker correctly.
- Pay attention to the speaker's tone, emotions, and body language along with their words.  

## 3. Reflection  

**Question 3:** What are the obstacles in your listening process?  

**Answer:**  
- Sometimes I get distracted by my own thoughts.
- Thinking about my reply while listening.
- I may lose concentration when the topic is not interesting or the discussion is too long.  

**Question 4:** What can you do to improve your listening?  

**Answer:**  
- Avoid distractions.
- Focus on the speaker.
- Take notes when needed.
- Ask questions if I don't understand.  


## 4. Types of Communication  


**Question 5:** When do you switch to Passive communication style in your day to day life?  

**Answer:**  
When I want to avoid conflict, I sometimes stay quiet and let others decide.  

**Question 6:** When do you switch into Aggressive communication styles in your day to day life?  

**Answer:**  
When I am frustrated or feel ignored, I may become impatient or raise my voice.  

**Question 7:** When do you switch into Passive Aggressive communication styles in your day to day life?  

**Answer:**  
When I don't express my feelings directly and show them through silence, sarcasm, or gossip  

**Question 8:** How can you make your communication assertive?  

**Answer:**  
- Say what I feel clearly.
- Speak honestly without being rude.
- Respect other people's opinions.
- Stay calm during disagreements.
- Say “no” politely when needed.
