# Grit and Growth Mindset

## Question 1:

### Paraphrase (summarize) the video in a few (1 or 2) lines. Please feel free to use your own words.

Angela Duckworth says success is not only about talent or intelligence. Grit means working hard, staying patient, and not giving up when things get difficult.

## Question 2:

### Paraphrase (summarize) the video in a few (1 or 2) lines in your own words.

A person can improve through practice and effort. Having a growth mindset helps us learn from mistakes and become better.

## Question 3: 

### What is the Internal Locus of Control? What is the key point in the video?
### Internal Locus of Control:
It means believing that our actions and efforts have a major effect on our results.

### Key Points:

•&nbsp; Praising effort increases motivation.

•&nbsp; Hard work makes children more willing to face challenges.

•&nbsp; Praising only intelligence can reduce motivation.

•&nbsp; Children may avoid difficult tasks if they are only called “smart”.

•&nbsp; Effort and persistence are important for success.


## Question 4: 

### What does the speaker mention as the key points to build a growth mindset (explanation not needed).
### Key Points:

•&nbsp; Set a clear goal.

•&nbsp; Find out what skills you need.

•&nbsp; Decide how to learn them.

•&nbsp; Give time for learning.

•&nbsp; Practice regularly.
