## Focus Management

### Question 1  
What is Deep Work?  
**Ans :**  
* Doing one task at a time.  
* No phone, no social media. 
* Start with a Calm mind with no external thoughts. 
* Fully focus on what you are doing.  
* Helps you do efficient work.  
---
### Question 2  
According to the author, how to do deep work properly?  
**Ans :**  
* Stay focusing without getting distracted.  
* Use the phone only at planned times. 
* Keep track of things which you are doing. 
* Learn to stay calm.  
* Plan your whole day. 
* Set the goals to be completed. 
* Protect your time like it's very important.  
* Say "no" to things that take away your focus.  
---
### Question 3  
How can you use these ideas in daily life?  
**Ans :**  

* Use a planner to schedule your tasks.   
* Be positive about the things you do.
* Turn off phone notifications or use Do Not Disturb.  
* Make a quiet workspace with no mess or noise.  
* Start things with a good mood.
* Do only one task at a time.  
* Slowly increase how long you can stay focused.    
* Track how much time you focus every day.  
* Every Day, ask yourself What helped me? What distracted me?

### Question 4  
What are the dangers of social media?  
**Ans :**  
* **It makes you addicted.** You keep use social media everytime like eating , in bathroom , walking , travelling , talking to someone, driving a car or waiting on a traffic signal.
* **You may feel inferior.** You may compare yourself with people online and feel that their lives are better, happier than yours. 
* **Having unrealistic expectations.** By seeing Photos, lifestyles, celebrations, and beauty shown online , you may expect the same from others and get disapointment or feel ungrateful for what you have.
* **It can make you feel sad.** People usally post their best moments on social media(LinkedIn , Instagram , etc)so seeing their 
'happy life' can make you feel that your own life has a lot of problems.
* **Children expose to adult content.** Children may accidentally get exposed to nudity, violent, or other inappropriate content.
* **Effects your mental State.** more usage can make you feel Empty or make you feel lonely.  
* **It can lead to cyberbullying** People may use social media to threaten or harass others.
* **Reduces your attention span.** You get used to youtube shorts or insta reels  and can’t have long time focus on a single thing.
